You may not need it

The web app is the full app, on iPhone and iPad too. Open it in your browser and add it to your home screen. On iPhone and iPad that's Safari, then Share, then Add to Home Screen. This Android app is an optional extra.

What the app adds

It does everything the web app does, in both modes: the hosted service with no API key, or your own model. Both keep your latest digests for offline listening. On a phone the app adds three things:

It downloads new digests in the background, before you open it. The web app only fetches while it is open.

The digest keeps playing reliably with the screen off. You control it from the lock screen and your headphones.

You sign in with Amber on the same phone. There's no QR code to scan, and nalgorithm never sees your key.

Updates

Zapstore and Obtainium keep the app up to date. Both read new releases from GitHub.
